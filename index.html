About the project

Health360 — Patient Risk & Lifestyle Insights

An academic data-visualization project by Aadarsh Barkiya. The website brings two published Tableau Public visualizations together in a single, responsive interface.

Tableau PublicData visualizationHealthcare analyticsInteractive storytelling
Important contextThis project explores patterns in a dataset for educational and analytical purposes. It is not a diagnostic or clinical decision-support tool. Observed associations do not establish cause and effect, and the visualizations should not be used to make individual healthcare decisions.
